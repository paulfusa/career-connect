# Acceptance Test 24.1

**Story:** US-24: Resume AI Autofill & Review (#91)
**Feature covered:** AI Resume Reviewer (the required Generative AI feature)
- A job seeker can get written AI feedback on an uploaded resume.
- The review can be tailored to a job posting.
- The review is saved and is only visible to its owner.
- Clear messages are shown when the AI can't run.

### Given
- The AI is set up as in `docs/ai-setup.md` (local Ollama or a hosted provider) and the dev server was restarted.
- I am logged in as Ana, a job seeker, who has uploaded a text PDF resume, "Ana Resume.pdf".
- A published job posting exists, "Junior Developer" at Acme.
- A second job seeker, Carl, has an account.

### When
1. On **Resumes**, I click **Review** next to "Ana Resume.pdf".
2. I leave "General review" selected and click **Review my resume**, then wait for the result.
3. I go back to **Resumes**, then open the review again.
4. I choose "Junior Developer" in **Tailor the review to a job posting** and click **Review again**.
5. I copy the review page's address, log in as Carl and open it.
6. As Ana, I stop the AI (quit Ollama, or set a wrong `AI_API_KEY` and restart), then click **Review again**.

### Then
- [ ] While it runs, the button says "Reviewing…" with a note about how long it takes (a minute or two on a local model).
- [ ] The review shows **Overall**, **What works**, **What to improve**, **Stronger bullet points** and **Keywords worth adding**.
- [ ] Each suggested bullet point sits next to the original line, and describes the same work without adding tools or numbers that aren't in the resume.
- [ ] The page ends with "Generated by AI (…) on <date>. It can be wrong or miss context, so treat it as a second opinion."
- [ ] Back on **Resumes**, the link now says **See review**, and reopening it shows the saved review without running the AI again.
- [ ] The tailored review adds **Fit for Junior Developer at Acme** and **In the posting but not in your resume**.
- [ ] Carl gets a "not found" page for Ana's review.
- [ ] With the AI stopped, an error says "The AI model isn’t responding. Check that Ollama is running, then try again." (or "The AI service refused the request…" for a wrong key), and the previous review is still shown.

### Result


# Acceptance Test 24.2

**Story:** US-24: Resume AI Autofill & Review (#91)
**Feature covered:** Fill profile from resume (the additional original feature)
- AI reads an uploaded resume and suggests profile details, experience with dates and bullet points, education and skills.
- Nothing is added to the profile until the user confirms, and existing data is never overwritten silently.
- Imported data passes the same validation as the profile forms.

### Given
- The AI is set up as in Acceptance Test 24.1.
- I am logged in as Ana, a job seeker, whose profile has a location ("Montréal"), one experience ("Developer intern" at Shopify) and the skill "Python".
- Ana has uploaded "Ana Resume.pdf", which lists the Shopify internship, another job, two schools, and several skills including Python.
- I also have "scan.pdf", a scanned resume saved as an image-only PDF.

### When
1. On **Resumes**, I click **Fill profile** next to "Ana Resume.pdf", then **Read my resume**, and wait.
2. I look at the checklist without changing anything.
3. I untick one of the schools and click **Add to profile**.
4. I open **Fill profile** for "Ana Resume.pdf" again.
5. I upload "scan.pdf", click **Fill profile** next to it, then **Read my resume**.

### Then
- [ ] While it runs, the button says "Reading…" with a note about how long it takes.
- [ ] The checklist shows **About you**, **Experience**, **Education** and **Skills** with what's in the resume: job titles, companies, start and end months, and bullet points.
- [ ] Fields that are empty on my profile are ticked; Location is unticked and shows "Replaces: Montréal".
- [ ] The Shopify internship is greyed out with "Already on your profile".
- [ ] **Skills** offers only the new skills ("Add N new skills"), not Python.
- [ ] Nothing has been added to my profile at this point.
- [ ] After **Add to profile**, I land on my profile with "Profile saved.": the new job with its dates and bullet points, the ticked school, and the new skills are there; the unticked school is not, Location is still "Montréal", and Shopify is not duplicated.
- [ ] Opening **Fill profile** again shows the saved result straight away, with the imported items now marked "Already on your profile".
- [ ] "scan.pdf" is refused with "This PDF has no readable text. It may be a scan or an image; export it from your editor as a text PDF."
